# 7 — Sources

Covers brief section 31.

> **How these sources were used.** The research environment's egress policy blocked direct HTTP
> access to `ato.gov.au`, `legislation.gov.au`, `austlii.edu.au` and every other external host.
> Primary-source content was therefore obtained through **search-engine retrieval of those same
> primary documents** — the search index returns excerpts of the ATO Legal Database, AustLII,
> Treasury, IRAS, the UAE Ministry of Finance and so on. Citations below point to the primary
> instrument and give the canonical URL, but **the full text of no instrument was read
> end-to-end**. Every material conclusion should be checked against the instrument itself. The
> three most important such checks are listed in the README.

|---|---|

### Sources deliberately not relied upon

Consistent with the brief: Reddit, YouTube, crypto tax blogs, relocation marketing sites,
citizenship-by-investment brokers and exchange marketing material were treated **only as leads**
to primary sources, never as authority. Where a jurisdiction's treatment is asserted in this
report, it is traceable to a statute, a revenue authority publication, or a professional firm's
technical analysis of one.
